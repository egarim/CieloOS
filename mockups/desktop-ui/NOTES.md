# CieloOS desktop mockup notes

- **The agent's state lives beside the apps, not inside one.** “Mira is working” and requests waiting for a decision stay visible from the desktop. I would test whether this feels reassuring or creates too much ambient pressure.
- **Permission is an interruption, not a feed item.** The live request uses a familiar, balanced two-choice dialog; the compact version in the right shelf handles a queue without turning it into an administrative inbox. I would test comprehension of the one-line reason and whether people notice the expandable detail.
- **Desk means the live screen; Activity means the human-readable history.** These are deliberately separate apps even though both describe the agent's work. I would test whether people understand where to go to watch versus where to verify something later.
- **First-run coexists with the desktop.** Rather than blocking the whole product with a wizard, the empty-provider state is a prominent desktop card and a fuller Settings view. I would test this against a blocking first-run flow, especially for people who want to look around before providing a key.

At 1280×800 I would specifically test the relationship between the live permission dialog, the right-hand work shelf, and the setup card; at 1920×1080 I would test whether the deliberately open canvas feels calm rather than unfinished.
